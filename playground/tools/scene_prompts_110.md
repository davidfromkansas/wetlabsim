# 110 example scene prompts, by persona

Test set for "Describe a scene". Each prompt is tagged by style:
**[short]** short and vague · **[detailed]** long and detailed · **[missing]** asks for things we likely don't have yet.

---

## 1. AI researcher comparing models (16)

1. [detailed] A plain table with 6 colored blocks and 3 bins. The Franka sorts the blocks by color, one per bin.
2. [detailed] Stack 4 cubes into a tower on a white table. One Franka, cubes start scattered.
3. [short] Simple pick and place test for a UR5e.
4. [detailed] 10 everyday objects on a table and a basket. Put only the fruit in the basket.
5. [detailed] A drawer cabinet on a bench with a mug inside the top drawer. Open the drawer, take out the mug, close the drawer.
6. [detailed] Two identical tables side by side, each with a PiPER and the same 5 objects, so I can compare two models fairly.
7. [short] Hardest tabletop clutter you can make for a single arm.
8. [detailed] A bowl, a plate and a cup on a table. Put the cup on the plate, then the plate in the bowl's place.
9. [detailed] A shelf with three levels. Move every can from the bottom shelf to the top shelf.
10. [detailed] Rearrange 5 objects on a table to match a line from smallest to largest.
11. [short] A long-horizon kitchen task with at least 6 steps.
12. [detailed] Door-opening benchmark: a cabinet with a hinged door and a microwave. Open both.
13. [detailed] Put the red block in the box, but only if the box lid is open. Lid starts closed.
14. [detailed] An ALOHA with a towel and a box. Fold the towel in half and put it in the box.
15. [short] Same scene as LIBERO kitchen but with different objects.
16. [missing] A table with a Rubik's cube. Solve it.

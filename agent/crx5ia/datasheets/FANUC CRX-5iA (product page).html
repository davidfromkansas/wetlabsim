The CRX-5iA Cobot was made with the intention of working safely and efficiently alongside human staff. With a 5kg payload and 994mm reach, the CRX-5iA cobot is perfect for manufacturers with little-to-no experience in robotics and automation, especially in applications like material handling, machine tending, and picking/packaging. The CRX series offers an all-new FANUC programming interface with simple drag-and-drop technology on a touchscreen teach pendant. 

The FANUC CRX-5iA is a safe, reliable cobot that can work with people in manufacturing settings. It's built with FANUC's trusted technology and is easy to program, even for beginners. The CRX series of cobots offers 8 Years Zero Maintenance, with the proven industrial reliability that FANUC customers expect. For applications where sanitation is an absolute must, the CRX-5iA is also offered in a food-grade cobot model.
